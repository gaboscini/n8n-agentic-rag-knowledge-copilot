# Refund and Cancellation Policy

Monthly subscriptions may be cancelled before the next renewal date. Service continues until the paid period ends. Partial-month refunds are not provided except where required by law or approved after a confirmed duplicate charge.

Annual subscriptions may receive a full refund when cancellation is requested within 30 calendar days of the initial purchase. Renewal payments are refundable only when the request is received within 14 calendar days of renewal and no material usage occurred after renewal.

Support may explain the policy and prepare a billing-review ticket. Support must not approve refunds. Finance Operations owns the decision and must record the approval reason.

Security incidents, service credits, and contractual remedies are handled under the applicable agreement and must not be interpreted as automatic cash refunds.
