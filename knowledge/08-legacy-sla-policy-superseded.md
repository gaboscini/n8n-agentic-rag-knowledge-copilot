# Legacy Priority Support SLA

This document is superseded. It stated that Enterprise SEV-1 incidents received a one-hour initial response and that Business SEV-1 incidents received a four-hour response.

Do not use these targets for current cases. Refer to Support Plans and Entitlements version 3.2 or later.
