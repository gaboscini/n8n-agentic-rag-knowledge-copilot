# Support Plans and Entitlements

The SaaS platform offers Starter, Business, and Enterprise subscriptions.

## Starter

- Support channel: email and customer portal
- Coverage: Monday to Friday, 09:00-17:00 customer region time
- Initial response: SEV-2 within 8 business hours; SEV-3 within 2 business days; SEV-4 within 3 business days
- SEV-1 incidents are not eligible because Starter environments are not designated production-critical

## Business

- Support channel: email, portal, and in-product support
- Coverage: Monday to Friday, 08:00-20:00 customer region time
- Initial response: SEV-1 within 2 hours during coverage; SEV-2 within 4 hours; SEV-3 within 1 business day; SEV-4 within 2 business days

## Enterprise

- Support channel: email, portal, in-product support, and priority incident channel
- Coverage: 24x7 for SEV-1 and SEV-2; business hours for SEV-3 and SEV-4
- Initial response: SEV-1 within 30 minutes; SEV-2 within 2 hours; SEV-3 within 8 business hours; SEV-4 within 2 business days
- Enterprise customers may request a named incident manager for SEV-1 incidents

Support entitlement must be confirmed from the customer record before communicating an SLA. Contract-specific overrides stored on the customer account take precedence.
