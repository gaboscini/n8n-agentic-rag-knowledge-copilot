# Incident Severity and Escalation Matrix

## SEV-1: Critical

A production service is unavailable for most users, critical data processing has stopped, or there is a confirmed security incident with material customer impact. No reasonable workaround exists.

Required actions:

1. Assign the Incident Response team immediately.
2. Notify the incident manager.
3. Open the priority Slack incident channel.
4. Record the customer impact, start time, affected regions, and known workaround.
5. Provide updates at least every 30 minutes for Enterprise customers.

## SEV-2: High

A major feature is unavailable, a substantial user group is affected, or performance degradation materially blocks business operations. A limited workaround may exist.

Assign Platform Support and notify the account owner when the customer is Enterprise.

## SEV-3: Normal

A non-critical feature is impaired, the impact is limited, or an acceptable workaround exists. Assign Product Support.

## SEV-4: Low

General questions, documentation corrections, cosmetic defects, and feature requests. Assign Customer Support.

The agent may recommend a severity but must not silently downgrade a customer-reported SEV-1. A human incident manager owns final SEV-1 classification.
