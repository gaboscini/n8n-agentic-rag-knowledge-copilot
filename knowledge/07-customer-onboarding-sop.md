# Enterprise Customer Onboarding SOP

Enterprise onboarding includes a kickoff, identity configuration, integration review, data-migration plan, administrator training, production-readiness review, and handoff to Customer Success.

The support team owns troubleshooting after the production handoff. The implementation team owns configuration during onboarding. Security questions requiring contractual interpretation must be assigned to Security Assurance or Legal.

Production readiness requires:

- named customer administrator;
- verified domains and SSO configuration;
- approved integration inventory;
- tested backup administrator access;
- incident and escalation contacts;
- data-retention selections;
- successful non-production validation.
