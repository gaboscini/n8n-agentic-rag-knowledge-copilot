# Support Data Handling Policy

Support personnel may request sanitized logs, request identifiers, timestamps, tenant IDs, and redacted screenshots when required for troubleshooting.

Never request or store:

- passwords or one-time passcodes;
- full access tokens, refresh tokens, or session cookies;
- private cryptographic keys;
- complete payment-card data;
- unrestricted database exports;
- identity documents unless an approved verification process explicitly requires them.

Secrets accidentally received in a ticket must be treated as compromised. Instruct the customer to revoke or rotate the secret, restrict access to the ticket, and notify Security Operations.

Customer content may be used only for the active support purpose. Copying it into public channels, personal tools, or unapproved AI services is prohibited.
